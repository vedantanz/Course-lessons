What we left open

A filter, and no history for it

The essay The Groove That Runs Itself gave you three numbers: overshoot, settling time, residual ripple. It let you set them by hand and watch what each combination did to an ordinary day.

It never asked how a real filter gets its settings. Nobody chooses their own overshoot. Nobody decides, on purpose, to carry residual ripple, a hum with no trigger, through an otherwise ordinary afternoon.

So where do the numbers come from, and is anything about that still in your hands?

Nothing has happened to him

Arun's ordinary Tuesday

Arun is thirty-six. His job is fine. His marriage is fine. He wakes most mornings already faintly braced, for what he could not say. He checks his phone before his feet are on the floor, works through a feed in every gap of the day, and ends most nights the same way, close to midnight, the last thing his eyes do before they close.

There was no roster incident. No sharp word he regrets. Just a low hum of unease with nothing obvious attached to it, and no memory of when that became true.

That is residual ripple. This essay is about how a person arrives there with nothing dramatic to blame.

The first mechanism

Repetition, spaced, not massed

One of the most robust findings in memory research is the spacing effect: the same amount of repetition produces a far stronger, more durable trace when spread across time than when delivered all at once. Thirty-eight repetitions over three days can outlast sixty-eight crammed into one sitting.

Arun's morning unease was not caused by one bad morning. It was caused by the same low-grade input, delivered the same way, at spaced intervals, for months. That is exactly the schedule that produces the strongest trace of all.

The second mechanism

Why the cue never resembles the cause

A cue reopens an old disposition not by resembling its content, but by overlapping the state the disposition was formed in. A tone of voice. A kind of silence. A particular hour of the evening.

This is why Arun cannot point to what set off any one bad day. The cue was never going to look like the cause. It only had to match it.

The third mechanism

A thousand small waves, one shape

Psychologists call the structure that forms from many particular experiences a schema: an abstraction that organises how every new experience is met, before it is consciously processed.

Arun does not have a thousand separate memories of checking his phone. Those small acts stopped being separate long ago. They became one structure, a schema for how a day begins, met before he has any chance to notice it operating.

In plain words

A disposition is not one groove. It is many grooves that have fused into a single orientation.

Honesty line

What kind of claim this is

The spacing effect, cue-dependent retrieval, and schema formation are established science, each with named researchers behind it.

Structural model

What follows, reading this science alongside a fifteen-hundred-year-old account of how disposition accumulates, is a bridge this course draws for insight. It is not a claim that either side proves the other.

The point of the last two slides

Not settings. Results.

In the essay The Groove That Runs Itself, overshoot, settling time and residual ripple were dials you moved. Here they are outcomes, the end of thirty days of a particular mix, and nobody sat down and chose to build a filter like that.

A month of quiet, steady input and a month of conflict do not produce two moods. They produce two different filters, and the filter is what meets every day after.

Vivekananda, on the wave

It only becomes fine. It does not disappear.

Whatever work we do, the mind is thrown into a wave, and after the work is finished, we think the wave is gone. No. It has only become fine, but it is still there. Swami Vivekananda, on Yoga Sūtra II.12

This is the intuition the interactive just overturned. What you are not thinking about has not finished. It becomes fine, below the threshold of notice, and it waits.

Yoga Sūtra IV.9

Experiences becoming fine, become impressions

jāti-deśa-kāla-vyavahitānām apy ānantaryaṁ smṛti-saṁskārayor eka-rūpatvāt

There is consecutiveness in desires, even though separated by species, space, and time, there being identification of memory and impressions. Vivekananda's rendering

Memory and impression share one form. That is why a cue with no resemblance to the original event can still reopen it. The trigger was never going to look like the cause.

Four conditions an impression can be in

Dormant. Attenuated. Overpowered. Expanded.

"Overpowered" means that sometimes one set of impressions is held down for a while by those that are stronger, but they come out when that repressing cause is removed. Vivekananda, on Yoga Sūtra II.4

Dormant, present but wholly inactive. Attenuated, weakened by sustained practice. Overpowered, held down by something stronger, ready to return the moment that pressure lifts. Expanded, given the conditions it needs, and fully active.

Hold on to overpowered. It is not the same as changed, and the difference matters later in this series.

Yoga Sūtra IV.10

No traceable beginning, and no need for one

Thirst for happiness being eternal, desires are without beginning. All experience is preceded by desire for happiness. There was no beginning of experience, as each fresh experience is built upon the tendency generated by past experience. Vivekananda's rendering

Go looking for the first impression and you will not find it, not because it is hidden, but because the looking itself presupposes a mind already shaped enough to look. Arun does not need to locate where this began. There may not be a beginning to find.

Yoga Sūtra IV.8

The great check on your own history

Desires can only manifest themselves in proper environments. Only those desires will come out for which the environment is fitted; the rest will remain stored up. This shows that the power of environment is the great check to control even Karma itself. Vivekananda's rendering

Not every stored impression gets to act at once. Which ones become active depends on what the surroundings permit. Change the environment, and you change which parts of your own history get a chance to run.

The honest second half

Not the building. The people.

Every day people go there, and the more they go the holier they get, and the holier that place becomes... But you must remember that their holiness depends on holy people congregating there. It is not the building, but the people that make a church. Vivekananda, on Yoga Sūtra II.19

Environment shapes disposition, and environment is itself made by people, so the causation runs both ways. This is not superstition about a place having power in itself. It is a claim about sustained exposure, over time, to a certain quality of surroundings.

Before this goes further

This is not a verdict

Please read this one carefully

This is an account of how disposition forms through ordinary, repeated, unremarkable input. It is not an account of why any particular person is suffering, and it is not a diagnosis. If you live with depression, anxiety, or any other condition, you did not simply feed it to yourself.

Disposition is shaped by biology, circumstance, and events entirely outside anyone's choosing. What this essay offers is a lever available to you now, among many other things not fully within your control. It is not a verdict on your past.

The one hour that matters most

What you carry into sleep

Sleep does not pause the day. It actively consolidates whatever was encoded just before it, and this process prioritises emotionally charged material over neutral material.

Whatever occupies the last portion of your attention before sleep is not one input among many. It is the input your mind is about to spend several hours strengthening, with less competition and with emotional material given priority.

Of everything changeable in Arun's day, the last hour is very likely the highest-leverage one to change first.

Yoga Sūtra IV.11

Why this is ongoing, not a fix

So long as the senses receive the external objects, fresh desires will arise... the mind-stuff is the great storehouse, the support of all past desires reduced to Samskāra form; until they have worked themselves out, they will not die. Vivekananda's rendering

The inflow never stops on its own, because a mind with senses will always be receiving something. This is not a reason to give up on managing it. It is the reason the practice continues.

What to carry out of this

The filter was not born fully formed. It is being built, today, by what you take in and when.

  • Repetition, spaced across time, is what a disposition is made of.
  • A cue reopens the past by matching its form, not by resembling its content.
  • There is no findable beginning, and none is needed to begin working with it.
  • Environment governs which of your stored tendencies get to act.
  • What formed you is not a verdict on you.
  • The last hour before sleep is the highest-leverage hour to change first.

Some part of what shaped this filter, you did not choose. Some part of what feeds it now, you do.
